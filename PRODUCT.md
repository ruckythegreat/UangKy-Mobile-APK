# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indonesian students and young adults learning to budget and track personal money. They open UangKy on their phone — often between classes, after a purchase, or at the end of the week — to log what came in and went out, check balances across multiple books, and understand cash flow without opening a spreadsheet or a bank app.

## Product Purpose

UangKy is a lightweight, local-first personal finance notebook: multi-ledger tracking, transactions, recurring schedules, reports, and CSV export. Data stays on-device. Success means users trust the numbers they see, log transactions quickly, and build a budgeting habit that feels grown-up — not gamified, not intimidating.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, minimal, trustworthy. Voice is direct and precise (Indonesian UI copy). The interface should feel like a competent tool that respects the user's intelligence — editorial restraint, tight hierarchy, no decorative noise. Warmth comes through the peach accent and careful touches, not through soft, saturated backgrounds or playful gimmicks.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS finance dashboards: cream/sand body backgrounds, hero-metric cards, gradient accents, small uppercase eyebrow kickers above every section, identical icon+heading+text card grids.
- Gamified budgeting apps with coins, streaks, mascots, or reward loops that undermine seriousness.
- Corporate bank apps that feel cold, dense, and institutional.
- Neon fintech aesthetics (dark + electric accent, crypto-bro energy).

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers earn trust** — balances, trends, and reports are shown plainly; no decorative metrics or vanity stats.
2. **Restraint over decoration** — every element justifies its presence; remove before adding.
3. **Hierarchy does the work** — type scale, spacing, and alignment carry meaning; color is accent, not scaffolding.
4. **Peach is accent, not wallpaper** — brand warmth lives in touches (buttons, indicators, logo moments), not in saturated body backgrounds.
5. **Motion respects focus** — transitions are purposeful and brief; always provide a reduced-motion alternative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, readable type on phone screens in bright ambient light. Extra care for `prefers-reduced-motion` (crossfade or instant, never gated content visibility). Charts and income/expense indicators must remain distinguishable for common color-vision deficiencies — do not rely on red/green alone.
